About

Dr. Farah Elgharroudi

Specialist in Endocrinology, Diabetology and Nutrition, Dr. Elgharroudi puts her expertise at the service of her patients for comprehensive and personalized care.

A graduate of the Faculty of Medicine and Pharmacy of Marrakech, she combines advanced medical skills and attentive listening to support each patient in their care journey.
